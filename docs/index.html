Shot Quality & Shot Making

xFG% is the chance an average NBA shooter makes a given shot, from where it's taken and how (dunk, layup, pull-up, step-back). Subtract it from a player's real FG% and you separate good shot selection from good shooting. The model is trained on 2014-15 to 2022-23 and evaluated on the three seasons after. Code on GitHub

Where shots come from

League-wide, test seasons. 58% of attempts are either at the rim or above-the-break threes.

On unseen seasons the model reaches ROC-AUC 0.64 and a Brier score of 0.228, against 0.249 for always guessing the average. Gradient boosting on the same features adds only about 0.01 AUC, so without defender or shot-clock data a linear model is close to the ceiling. Open the player-vs-league dashboard.

Data: NBA Stats ShotChartDetail via nba_api, regular seasons 2014-15 to 2025-26, end-of-quarter heaves over 40 ft removed. Method and limitations in the README.
